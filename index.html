The Craft Certification Project

Helping artists distinguish their work from slop.

What will this be? The goal is to create a simple way for artists to "Craft" certify their works or endeavours.
What would this look like? Ideally a small, recognizable seal or badge with a reference code that someone could look up and verify. Somewhere in-between a Motion Pictures Association/Parental Advisory sticker.
Why not just certify my own work? AI scammers are relentless- they don't care about their work or audience and will happily burn down a rainforest to generate "proof" their slop is the product of their own imagination. Our goal is help overcome this confusion.
How would this work? In theory we would help certify individual artistic endevaours- such as an album or a film. We would involve subject experts and talk to people to guarantee a work.
Who is running this? Currently this is an idea by me- artist and illustrator Alex Tomlinson. I want to get people smarter than me together to really make this happen!
